# TripWise

TripWise is a fishing decision app for small-scale fishers in Navotas and Manila Bay. The job is simple: help someone decide if a trip is worth the diesel and safe enough to come home.

## Who it is for

Municipal fishers running a small bangka. Many are more comfortable in casual Filipino than formal English. They work from the coast, often with weak signal, and they already know the water. What they need is a fast read: weather, waves, diesel, and whether the last trip paid.

## Product rules

- Safety beats profit. If the sea is dangerous, the call is STAY. Never soften that.
- GO / CAUTION / STAY stay visible in both English and Filipino. Keep the codes. Translate the explanation around them.
- Numbers people can check: diesel, liters, kilos, pesos. No black box.
- Offline-first. Cached weather and last ledger stay usable when the signal drops.
- Language is spoken 2026 Filipino, not poetic or textbook Tagalog. Taglish is fine.

## Surfaces

Today (score and economics), Weather, Map and location share, Catch log / ledger, History, What-If, and Settings (language, motor, ground, family SMS).
